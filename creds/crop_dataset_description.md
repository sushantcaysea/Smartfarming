# Crop Recommendation Dataset Feature Descriptions

This document provides a detailed description of each feature (column) in the `Crop_recommendation.csv` dataset, formatted for inclusion in your project report.

| Feature Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Nitrogen (N)** | Ratio of Nitrogen content in the soil (measured in mg/kg or ppm). Nitrogen is a primary nutrient essential for leaf development, vegetative growth, and chlorophyll production, giving plants their green color. |
| **Phosphorus (P)** | Ratio of Phosphorus content in the soil (measured in mg/kg or ppm). It is vital for energy transfer, root development, early plant growth, and the production of flowers and seeds. |
| **Potassium (K)** | Ratio of Potassium content in the soil (measured in mg/kg or ppm). Potassium regulates water utilization (opening and closing of stomata), improves disease resistance, and enhances overall crop quality and yield. |
| **Temperature** | Ambient air temperature of the environment (measured in degrees Celsius, °C). It directly affects the rate of photosynthesis, transpiration, seed germination, and enzyme activity within the crop. |
| **Humidity** | Relative humidity level of the air (measured in percentage, %). High humidity reduces plant transpiration but can increase the risk of fungal leaf diseases, while low humidity accelerates water loss. |
| **pH** | The potential of Hydrogen (pH) of the soil, measuring its acidity or alkalinity on a scale from 0 to 14. Soil pH determines how easily essential nutrients dissolve and become available for absorption by crop roots. |
| **Rainfall** | Cumulative precipitation level of the cultivation region (measured in millimeters, mm). It serves as the primary source of water, supplying the moisture necessary for cell turgidity and nutrient transportation. |
| **Label** | The target variable (class label) representing the recommended crop category (e.g., Rice, Maize, Mango, Chickpea). This is the output class predicted by the machine learning classification model. |
